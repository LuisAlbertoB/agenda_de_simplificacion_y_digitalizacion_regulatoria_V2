## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Purpose
The design system serves as the foundational operating interface for the **Agenda de Simplificación y Digitalización Regulatoria** (aligned with the Ley Nacional de Eficiencia y Transformación Burocrática - LNETB and FASD 2026 for Tuxtla Gutiérrez). It bridges municipal institutional authority with high-velocity developer ergonomics. The emotional response is centered on **uncompromising precision, institutional trust, and digital clarity**, banishing bureaucratic fatigue through a refined, low-strain dark environment.

### Design Movement: Modern Dark GovTech & Structural Minimalism
The visual language merges **Material Design 3 dark tonal layering** with clean **structural minimalism** and subtle **ambient luminescence**:
- **Tone & Mood:** Deep navy-slate foundations prevent eye fatigue during high-volume regulatory audits and multi-attribute schema management.
- **Institutional Dignity:** Warm municipal gold (`#c6923b`) reflects the civic identity of Tuxtla Gutiérrez, balanced by analytical cyan (`#0284c7` / `#38bdf8`) for technological operations, and verified emerald (`#10b981`) for legal compliance validity.
- **Form Follows Function:** Dense data structures, Gantt schedules, and deep relational regulatory trees require deterministic layouts, crisp high-contrast data readouts, and permanent visual anchors for critical commitments.

## Colors

### Palette Strategy
The color architecture relies on a specialized high-contrast dark scheme engineered for administrative data density:
- **Base Canvas (`#0b0f19`):** Deepest void layer, absorbing ambient light and eliminating OLED/screen glare for desk audits.
- **Primary Institutional Gold (`#c6923b` / `#d97706`):** Reserved for institutional identity, master validation actions ("Guardar Ficha Oficial"), and active primary controls.
- **Secondary State Cyan (`#0284c7` / `#38bdf8`):** Represents digital transformational states, active schema navigation, query links, and analytical filters.
- **Tertiary Compliance Emerald (`#10b981`):** Applied exclusively to confirmed compliance, complete statutory milestones, and active API signals.
- **Tonal Hierarchy & Contrast:** Every interactive text and numeric metric meets or exceeds WCAG 2.1 AAA contrast ratios against dark container surfaces. Background elements utilize layered alpha blending over `#0b0f19` to convey spatial depth without relying on stark drop shadows.

## Typography

### Typography Strategy
Three high-legibility Google Fonts govern the typographic structure:
1. **Outfit (Headings & KPI Digits):** Geometric balance, modern presence, and razor-sharp clarity on oversized statistics and dashboard hero headers.
2. **Plus Jakarta Sans (UI Prose & Section Titles):** Humanist warmth and open counters, maximizing sustained reading comfort in long regulatory legal descriptions and administrative forms.
3. **Inter (Form Inputs, Labels & Numeric Data Tables):** Utilitarian, uniform character metrics designed for tabular currency values, CURP identifiers, database keys, and dense compliance check matrices.

## Layout & Spacing

### Grid & Fluid Rhythm
- **Fluid Grid Engine:** The system employs a 12-column responsive fluid grid bounded at `1440px` max-width (`mx-auto`). Standard gutter is `1.5rem` (24px) on desktop and `1rem` (16px) below `768px`.
- **Vertical Spacing Rhythm:** Built upon an 8-point structural system. Component gaps default to `0.5rem` (tight form controls), `1rem` (card action groups and toolbars), and `1.5rem` (dashboard widget separation).
- **Breakpoint Hierarchy:**
  - `sm` (640px): Stacked single-column form inputs and collapse of secondary navigation labels to icon-only.
  - `md` (768px): Dual-column field distribution and adaptive table wrapping.
  - `lg` (1024px): Full multi-column dashboard with persistent metadata rail.
  - `xl` (1280px): Extended Gantt viewports with horizontal kinetic overflow.
- **Sticky Action Bar Constraints:** Primary action bars (e.g., "Guardar Ficha Oficial", "Aprobar Semestre") are pinned with `sticky bottom-0 z-40`. They incorporate backdrop blur (`backdrop-blur-md`) and defensive safe-area padding (`pb-[env(safe-area-inset-bottom,1rem)]`) to ensure submit triggers never clip or render outside viewable limits across mobile and desktop devices.

## Elevation & Depth

### Tonal Hierarchy & Ambient Lighting
Depth is established primarily through **tonal layering and luminous borders**, avoiding dirty drop shadows:
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Pure `#0b0f19`. Hosts subtle radial ambient gradients (`rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.08)` and `rgba(198, 146, 59, 0.05)`) fixed to view extremities.
- **Level 1 (Structural Cards & Sections):** `rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.85)` with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` and `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`.
- **Level 2 (Recessed Wells & Form Surfaces):** `rgba(11, 15, 25, 0.65)` inset within Level 1 panels with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.
- **Level 3 (Interactive Hover & Popovers):** Elevated surface `#1e293b` with colored rim light (`0 0 0 1px rgba(198, 146, 59, 0.35)` or `0 0 0 1px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.35)`).
- **Level 4 (Modals & Command Sheets):** Pinned at `z-50` with an opaque dark backdrop `rgba(3, 7, 18, 0.8)` (`backdrop-blur-sm`), casting `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`.

## Shapes

### Shape Semantics
The design system operates with **Level 2 (Rounded)** curvature:
- **Micro Elements (Badges, Tags, Checkboxes):** `rounded-md` (6px–8px) to retain technical sharpness and compact footprint.
- **Input Controls & Buttons:** `rounded-lg` (8px–10px) to balance ergonomics with enterprise software aesthetics.
- **Containers & Stat Cards:** `rounded-xl` (14px–16px) creating cohesive card clusters.
- **Pill Exceptions:** Status badges and state indicators utilize `rounded-full` (9999px) for organic recognition of live processes.

## Components

### 1. Buttons & Persistent Sticky Triggers
- **Primary Institutional CTA ("Guardar Ficha Oficial"):**
  - Background: Gradient `from-[#c6923b] to-[#d97706]`.
  - Typography: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, 14px, weight 600, color `#ffffff`.
  - Interaction: Hover transforms `-1px` vertically with a warm gold halo `box-shadow: 0 4px 14px rgba(198, 146, 59, 0.35)`.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent base, `border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, text `#f8fafc`. Hover surface: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`.
- **Sticky Footer Action Bar:** Fixed container spanning full width with `bg-[#0b0f19]/90 backdrop-blur-md border-t border-white/10 p-4`, guaranteed flex-wrap layout preventing overflow on narrow mobile screens.

### 2. Form Inputs & Diagnostic Selectors
- **Fields (Input, Select, Textarea):**
  - Background: `rgba(11, 15, 25, 0.65)`.
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
  - Focus State: Outline-free, transitioning to `border-[#38bdf8]` with focus ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.25)`.
  - Typography: `Inter`, 14px, text `#f8fafc`.
- **Maturity Level Selector (Nivel de Digitalización 0–3):**
  - Segmented card grid displaying digit (`Outfit`, 24px) and title. Active card receives border `1px solid #10b981`, background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.1)`, and an emerald status pip.

### 3. KPI Stat Cards & Navigation Tabs
- **Stat Cards:** Deep glass background (`rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.75)`), internal padding `1.5rem`. Value rendered in `Outfit` 32px weight 700. Includes dedicated `40x40px` icon well with 10% opacity primary tint.
- **Tab Navigation:** Horizontal flex with `gap-2`. Active tab has border-b `2px solid #c6923b` and bold white text. Inactive tabs display muted slate `#94a3b8`. Integrated numeric pill counters dynamically show item quantities.

### 4. Regulatory Matrix, Gantt & Data Tables
- **Table Structure:** Headers in `Plus Jakarta Sans` 12px uppercase tracking `0.05em`, background `rgba(11, 15, 25, 0.8)`. Table rows feature `border-b border-white/5` with hover illumination `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)`.
- **Gantt Container:** Native horizontal smooth scrolling (`overflow-x-auto scroll-smooth`) containing milestone bars color-coded by institutional urgency and completion state.
